Your money, on your own computer.

tally links your bank, cards, and investments and shows where every dollar went. It runs on your machine, and the data never leaves it.

Why tally

Everything a money app does, minus the company in the middle

Banks connect through Plaid, the same service most finance apps use. Instead of going to someone's servers, your transactions land in one SQLite file you own.

Private by default

It listens on your own computer only. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no tally account to sign up for.

Updates itself

Every morning it syncs your accounts. Big charges, broken connections, and blown budgets can ping you on Discord.

Free to run

Plaid's Trial plan covers 10 bank connections at no cost. tally has no dependencies to buy or install beyond Node.

Budgets

See what's left before you spend it

Set a monthly limit per category, or fill them all in from your last three months. Each bar shows where today falls in the month, so you know if you're ahead or behind.

  • Over budget is always marked in pink, with the amount
  • Spending outside a budget still shows up
Spending

Where it went, month by month

Every category in one ring, with the last several months beside it. Tap a category to see the transactions behind it, or a month to look back at it.

  • Refunds lower spending instead of hiding
  • Card payments and transfers don't count twice
Activity

Every transaction, easy to fix

Search by name, amount, or category. Change a category for one charge, or for every charge from that merchant from now on.

  • Search ignores case and punctuation: "mcdonalds" finds McDonald's
  • Filter by account, category, or month
Bills and net worth

What's coming, and what you're worth

tally spots your recurring bills and subscriptions and lists what's due next. Accounts adds up cash, investments, and card balances into one number that it tracks over time.

  • Brokerages and retirement accounts included
  • Works on your phone through Tailscale
What you need

Four things, most of which you already have

A computer that stays onA Mac is easiest, since a script keeps tally running. Linux works too.
Node 24 or newertally uses Node's built-in SQLite. Nothing else to install.
A free Plaid accountPlaid's Trial plan gives 10 bank connections. US and Canada only.
Tailscale, if you want it on your phoneOptional. It reaches tally from your devices without opening it to the internet.
