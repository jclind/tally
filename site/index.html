A money app that runs on your own computer

tally links your bank, cards, and investments through Plaid and shows your spending, budgets, bills, and net worth. It stores everything in a SQLite file on your computer.

Set it up See the code

tally is free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads.

How it works

tally pulls your transactions from Plaid with your own API keys and stores them in a SQLite file on your computer.

No tally server

It listens on localhost, so only that computer can open it, or your own devices through Tailscale. There's no account to sign up for. Your bank data passes through Plaid, and alerts go to Discord if you turn them on.

Syncs on its own

It syncs every morning, and again when you open it if the data is old, so a laptop that slept through the morning catches up. If you turn on Discord alerts, it posts when a charge is at or above your alert amount, a budget goes over, or a bank stops syncing.

Costs nothing to run

Plaid's free Trial plan allows 10 bank connections in total, and removing one doesn't free its slot. Plaid offers it to accounts created on or after April 15, 2026.

Monthly budgets per category

Set a monthly limit per category, or fill them all in from your last three months. A tick on each bar marks today, so a bar past its tick means you're spending faster than the month is going.

  • A category over budget turns pink and shows how much it's over
  • Categories without a budget are listed with what you spent in each

Spending by category and month

A ring chart shows every category, with monthly totals for up to the last 12 months beside it. Tap a category to see the transactions behind it, or a month to look back at it.

  • A refund lowers its category's spending
  • Card payments and transfers between your accounts aren't counted as spending

Search and recategorize transactions

Search by name, amount, or category. Change a category for one charge, or for every charge from that merchant from now on.

  • Search ignores case and punctuation: "mcdonalds" finds McDonald's
  • Filter by account, category, or month

Recurring bills and net worth

tally lists the recurring bills and subscriptions Plaid detects, with the next due date for each. The Accounts page adds up cash, investments, and card balances into one number that it tracks over time.

  • Net worth includes brokerage and retirement accounts
  • Bills are grouped by the month they're due

What you need

A Mac or Linux computerIt doesn't have to stay on. tally syncs when it starts and when you open it. On a Mac, a script runs it in the background. Windows isn't supported.
Node 24 or newertally uses Node's built-in SQLite and has no npm dependencies.
A free Plaid accountPlaid's Trial plan allows 10 bank connections in total, for US and Canadian banks.
Tailscale, if you want it on your phoneOptional. It lets your phone reach tally without exposing it to the internet.
