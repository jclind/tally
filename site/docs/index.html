Before you start

  • A Mac or Linux computer. It doesn't have to stay on. tally syncs when it starts and when you open it, if the data is older than a setting you choose. A Mac is easiest, because a script runs it in the background. Linux works with any process manager.
  • Node 24 or newer. Check with node -v. Get it from nodejs.org or brew install node.
  • git, to download tally and update it later.
  • A US or Canadian bank. Plaid's free plan is only offered there.

1Get Plaid keys

Plaid is the service that connects to your bank. tally talks to it with your own keys, so your data goes from Plaid to your computer with no tally server in between.

  1. Create an account at dashboard.plaid.com/signup and verify your email.
  2. Open dashboard.plaid.com/trial-plan and fill in the Trial plan form. It's free and covers 10 bank connections with Transactions, Investments, and Liabilities, which is everything tally uses.
  3. Go to Developers → Keys. Copy your client_id and your Sandbox secret. You'll use the Production secret in step 4.
Don't apply for full Production access. Plaid ends the free Trial once you submit a Production application, and you can't go back.

Plaid offers the Trial to teams created on or after April 15, 2026. If your Plaid account is older, check the plans on your dashboard home page.

2Install tally

Download it and save your keys where tally looks for them, outside the code folder. Swap in your client_id and Sandbox secret before you run this:

git clone https://github.com/jclind/tally.git
cd tally
mkdir -p ~/.config/tally
cat > ~/.config/tally/plaid.env <<'EOF'
PLAID_CLIENT_ID=your-client-id
PLAID_SECRET=your-sandbox-secret
PLAID_ENV=sandbox
EOF
chmod 600 ~/.config/tally/plaid.env

Then start it:

npm start

The log line should end in (keys found). Open localhost:3400.

No npm install step. tally has no npm dependencies. npm test runs tally's checks against a fake Plaid.

3Link an account

Open Accounts and choose Link a bank or card, or Link a brokerage for investments. Plaid's window opens.

In sandbox, pick any bank and sign in with the test login user_good and password pass_good. tally pulls in Plaid's made-up accounts and transactions so you can look around.

4Switch to your real banks

To switch to real accounts, stop tally (Ctrl+C) and set the sandbox data aside:

mv data data-sandbox

In ~/.config/tally/plaid.env, change PLAID_SECRET to your Production secret and PLAID_ENV to production. Then start tally again with npm start.

Link your banks from Accounts the same way. The first sync brings in as much history as your bank gives Plaid, up to two years.

The Trial plan allows 10 connections in total. Removing one doesn't give the slot back. If a bank stops syncing, use Fix connection on the Accounts page instead of linking it again. tally shows how many you've used.

Some large banks sign you in on their own site (OAuth). If one won't connect, check Plaid's dashboard for an OAuth institutions page; a few banks need you to request access there first.

Keep it running

On a Mac, one script starts tally at login, restarts it if it stops, and runs the daily sync at 7:00:

bin/install-mac.sh

It installs two launchd agents, local.tally and local.tally-daily, pointing at this folder. The log is tally.log in the same folder. To restart tally after a change:

launchctl kickstart -k gui/$(id -u)/local.tally

On a laptop

tally syncs when it starts (at login, with the script) and when you open the page or switch back to its tab, if the data is older than Sync when you open tally on the Accounts page. The default is 12 hours. If the Mac is asleep at 7:00, launchd runs the daily sync when it wakes.

On Linux

Run node server.mjs under systemd or another process manager, and schedule node bin/daily.mjs for the morning with cron or a timer.

Use it on your phone

tally has no login, so it only listens on your computer. Tailscale lets your phone reach it over your tailnet without exposing it to the internet.

  1. Install Tailscale on the computer and your phone, and sign in to both with the same account.
  2. In Tailscale's admin console, under DNS, turn on MagicDNS and HTTPS certificates.
  3. On the computer, serve tally on port 8400 and tell tally its new address:
tailscale serve --bg --https=8400 http://127.0.0.1:3400
bin/install-mac.sh --host your-computer.your-tailnet.ts.net:8400

Open https://your-computer.your-tailnet.ts.net:8400 on your phone and add it to your home screen. tailscale status shows your computer's name.

Never use Tailscale Funnel or a port forward for tally. Either one puts your accounts on the public internet with no password.

Daily alerts

The 7:00 job syncs every account. If you give it a Discord webhook, it also posts to Discord for these:

  • a charge at or above your alert amount, $200 unless you change it in Settings
  • a budget that went over
  • a bank that stopped syncing (once a week until it's fixed)

In Discord, open a channel's settings, then Integrations → Webhooks, and copy the URL into a file:

echo 'DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL=https://discord.com/api/webhooks/...' > ~/.config/tally/discord.env
chmod 600 ~/.config/tally/discord.env

If nothing matches, it posts nothing. Without the file, alerts go to the log only.

Backups and updates

Back up two folders

Your accounts, transactions, settings, and the Plaid access tokens live in data/tally.db. Your Plaid keys and Discord webhook are in ~/.config/tally/, so back up both. Lose the database and you'd link every bank again, using up more of your 10 connections.

The daily job writes a snapshot to data/tally-snapshot.db with SQLite's VACUUM INTO, so it's safe to copy while tally runs. Copy that file and ~/.config/tally/ somewhere else, encrypted, on a schedule.

Update tally

git pull
launchctl kickstart -k gui/$(id -u)/local.tally

Using tally

Budgets

On Budgets, type a monthly amount next to any category. With no budgets yet, Fill them in sets every category from your last three months. The tick on each bar is today, so a bar past its tick is spending ahead of the month.

Fix a category

On Activity, click a transaction's category and pick another. Tick Every … transaction to apply it to that merchant from now on.

Search

Search finds names, amounts, and categories, and ignores case, spaces, and punctuation. mcdonalds finds McDonald's, 6.75 finds that exact charge, and groc finds Groceries.

Look back

The month name at the top of Home, Spending, and Budgets is a menu. Pick a past month to see all of it.

Settings

The Settings card at the bottom of Accounts has two choices. Sync when you open tally sets how old the data can get before opening tally syncs it: 1, 6, or 12 hours, a day, or never. Alert for one charge of sets the amount for the Discord alert.

Troubleshooting

The log says "keys missing"
tally reads ~/.config/tally/plaid.env. Check that the file exists and has both PLAID_CLIENT_ID and PLAID_SECRET.
A bank shows an error on the Accounts page
Its sign-in expired or changed. Click Fix connection and sign in again. This reuses the same connection and doesn't cost a slot.
A bank has only a few months of history
That's what the bank gives Plaid. Some send two years, some about 90 days.
The page won't load on my phone
Run tailscale serve status on the computer, and make sure the address you passed to --host matches it exactly, port included.
Something else
Open an issue on GitHub with the lines from tally.log. Leave out anything with your account details.
